Selvedge Garden is a place to drop the things that inspire you and come back to them later. Plant pictures, words, songs and links, and they land wherever they like.

Wildflowers sprout from what you've planted: finds from museums, libraries, poems, nature sightings and music, picked by plain rules, never by AI. There are no ads, no feeds and no algorithms working on your attention.

Share an allotment with friends, by invite only. Take cuttings from their gardens, or wander the public ones below.
